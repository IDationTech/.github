# CEEL

CEEL helps companies get and stay compliant with SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA and more. It connects to the tools a team already uses, checks controls automatically, and carries the company through its audit.

- **For your team:** one workspace where admins run the whole program, and an employee portal where staff sign policies and finish their tasks
- **For your auditor:** Audit Center, where auditors review evidence and run the audit
- **For your customers:** Trust Center, where buyers see your security posture
- **On every computer:** CEEL Device Agent, which checks that each device meets your policies
